# Jellyfin [PS] for PS4 - 1.13

## New, from the PS3 version

- **Music library tabs: Albums, Artists, Playlists, Genres and Songs.** L2 / R2 move between them. An artist or genre opens its albums, oldest first; a playlist plays in its own order. **Songs** lists every track, so music without an album tag can finally be found and played.
- **Shuffle and the queue.** In Now Playing, Triangle shuffles what is left (the track playing stays), the next three tracks show under UP NEXT, and Options opens the whole queue: pick any track and X plays it. X on a track in Songs or a playlist plays that list on from it; albums of more than 60 tracks now play to the end.
- **Live search, laid out as on the PS3.** The search field sits at the top with the keyboard under it, and the results update as you type, shown under the keyboard. Down from the keyboard's last row (or Options) folds it away and the results fill the screen: filter chips with their counts (All, Films, Shows, Episodes, Albums, Songs...) that L2 / R2 step through, and a box beside the list saying what the highlighted result is. Triangle opens its quick peek; Up from the first result, or Circle, brings the keyboard back. With nothing typed the list shows your last eight searches (X searches again, Square removes one). L1 / R1 change tab, as everywhere else; on the keyboard L2 / R2 move the cursor, and Circle clears the term (or leaves when it is empty).
- **Audio and subtitles while watching.** Triangle opens a menu of the film's audio tracks and subtitles (Left / Right switch between them); picking one carries on from the same moment with it.
- **Downloads.** Download (or Download season) on a film or episode's page saves it, one file at a time in the background. In a library, Options downloads whatever is highlighted: a film, an episode, a song, or all of an album, season or series. The Download button follows it - *Waiting to download*, *Downloading 45%*, *Downloaded* - and while anything downloads, its progress shows in the header beside the clock on every screen. **Downloads** (on Home, and in Settings › System) lists what is downloading, with a progress bar, and what is saved: X plays a download from its file, Square deletes it, Triangle pauses or resumes, Options stops it all. A paused, dropped or interrupted download carries on from where it stopped, even after the app is closed, and a dropped connection is retried by itself. Each file is checked for room first (5 GB is always left free on the console), and a file over 4 GB is not put on a FAT32 drive. Where you stop watching a download is kept beside it, so it resumes there, and sent to your server when it can be reached. **Settings › System › Save Downloads To** chooses the console or `JellyFin/` on a USB drive. To reach USB drives the app steps out of the PS4's app sandbox at launch with GoldHEN's own escape (GoldHEN 2.3 or later); without it, or without a drive, downloads go to the console.
- **USB drives, with no server.** Plug in a USB drive and a **USB Drive** card appears in My Media (and goes when the drive is pulled out). Browse its folders and play videos (MKV, MP4, AVI, TS and more) or music (MP3, FLAC, M4A, OGG, WAV and more) straight from it; a song plays its folder's music from that song, with shuffle and the queue. No sign-in is needed: **Play from a USB drive or downloads** on the first sign-in screen opens them, and if the server can't be reached Home still shows the USB drive and your downloads. Drives are read whether or not downloads are saved to USB, and need GoldHEN 2.3 or later.

## Fixed

- **Skip Intros and Skip Credits work the same on every account.** The intro and credits markers are asked for whether or not the account is an admin, and the settings are never locked. When the server has no markers for an episode, chapters named Intro, Opening, Recap, Credits or Ending are skipped instead.
- **Signing in no longer loops back to the sign-in screen on newer Jellyfin servers.** Jellyfin 10.12 turns off the older way of sending your sign-in with each request, so after "Loading Home..." the server rejected the app and it asked you to sign in again. The app now sends your sign-in the way current Jellyfin expects, and still works with older servers. ([#1](https://github.com/jw-notes/JellyFin-PS4/issues/1))
- **File sizes on the console are right.** Downloads and files on a USB drive showed the wrong size; the PS4's file information is now read the way it reports it.
