# Jellyfin [PS] for PS4 — 1.09

The first release of Jellyfin [PS] for PS4.

## Highlights

- Classic Home with My Media, Continue Watching, Next Up and Latest rows.
- Library grids with an A–Z jump bar, quick peek and background page loading.
- Full details page with backdrop, ratings, version / audio / subtitle choice and the cast as photo cards, with filmographies.
- Live TV channel grid with categories, favourites, recently watched, a three-hour guide and channel search.
- MPV/FFmpeg playback with an on-screen bar, scrubbing and five-minute skips.
- Artwork screensaver with on / off and delay settings.
- Quick Connect or username / password sign-in, saved per PS4 user.
- Startup animation, controller-icon hints, themes and an About page.

## Package

`dist/v1.09/IV0000-JFPS40000_00-JELLYFINPS400000.pkg` — title ID `JFPS40000`, version `01.09`.

## Known limitations

- Video is software-decoded at up to 720p; audio is stereo.
- Audio and subtitle tracks are chosen before playback, not during it.
- Live TV has no channel up / down inside the player.
